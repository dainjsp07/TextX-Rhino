  Copyright Google LLC and the Tesseract contributors. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/blob/main/LICENSE

Leptonica (x64\leptonica-*.dll)
  Copyright Dan Bloomberg and the Leptonica contributors. Licensed under the BSD 2-Clause style licence.
  http://leptonica.org/about-the-license.html

Tesseract language data (downloaded on first use from https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast)
  Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

Online services used without an account (only when you use the feature):
  Translation: the service chosen in Text X settings (default: Google Translate's free endpoint).
  Writing check: LanguageTool (https://languagetool.org), free public API; the text you check is sent to it.
